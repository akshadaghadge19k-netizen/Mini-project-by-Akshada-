## Algorithm
1. Start the program.
 
2. Create three linked lists: singly, circular, and doubly linked lists.

3. Display a menu of operations.

4. Accept the user's choice.

5. Insert a song into the selected linked list.

6. Delete a song by its name from the selected linked list.

7. Search for a song by its name.

8. Display all songs in the selected playlist.

9. Find the middle-position song:

10. Singly linked list: Traverse the list using slow and fast pointers.

11. Circular linked list: Traverse the circular list until the middle position is reached.

12. Doubly linked list: Count the nodes and traverse to the middle node.

13. Repeat the menu until the user chooses Exit.

14. Stop the program.
